FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is Handover?

A company brain on a graph. It tells you who owns a service now, and shows the path and the message behind that answer.

How does it know who owns something?

Ownership is an edge with a start date, an optional end date and a source message. Each answer is a fixed query on that graph, not a guess from text.

What happens when someone leaves?

A change event such as “Tunde left, Ada takes over” closes the old edges and opens new ones. Open tickets move to the new owner.

Is anything ever deleted?

No. Ownership is closed, not removed, so you can still ask who owned a service on any past date.

Where does the data live?

In FalkorDB. The demo uses Tidewell (sample workspace), a made-up company with sample people and services.

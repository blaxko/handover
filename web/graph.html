The graph

Nodes and edges, with dates.

Every ownership edge has a start, an optional end, and the message that proves it.

Nodes

Person

id, name, aliases, status (active or left).

Team

id, name.

Service

id, name, aliases. The things people own.

Ticket

id, name, status (open or closed).

Message

id, ts, channel, text. The provenance for every edge.

Edges

OWNS

Person → Service

valid_from, valid_to, source, closed_by. At most one open per service.

MEMBER_OF

Person → Team

valid_from, valid_to.

ASSIGNED_TO

Ticket → Person

valid_from, valid_to, reason, source.

DEPENDS_ON

Service → Service

What breaks when the thing below it breaks.

FOR · WORKED_ON · AUTHORED · ABOUT

Links

Ticket to service, person to service, person to message, message to service.

Four queries, as they run

T1 · who_owns

The owner of a service on a date. Edges are filtered by valid_from and valid_to, so history answers the same way as today.

{{T1}}

T4 · blast_radius

What a person owns now, the services that depend on it, and their open tickets. The dependency walk goes up to three hops.

{{T4}}

T5 · apply_handover

Records the event as a message, closes the old edges and opens the new ones in one request. Nothing is deleted.

{{T5}}

T6 · reassign_tickets

Moves open tickets from someone who left to the current owner. The new assignment starts on the event's effective date.

{{T6}}
